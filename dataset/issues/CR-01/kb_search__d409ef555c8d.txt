      "title": "Currency minor units and amount formatting",
      "body": "Solova stores every amount as an integer number of minor units (column suffix *Minor). Whenever an amount leaves the system (exports, reports, integrations) it is written in major units with exactly the currency's number of minor digits:\n- 2 digits: USD, EUR, GBP, AUD, CAD, SGD (12345 minor = 123.45; 10000 minor = 100.00).\n- 0 digits: VND, JPY, KRW (12345 minor = 12345).\nRules: '.' is the decimal point; no currency symbol; no thousands separator; no exponent; negative amounts use a leading '-'. Always keep the trailing zeros (100.00, not 100). Convert with integer arithmetic or the currency factor; never format with the base currency of the workspace when the invoice has its own currency. Amounts typed by a user are in the currency of the client or invoice they are typed for."
