      "title": "Invoice status codes",
      "body": "An invoice has exactly one status code: DRAFT, SENT, PAID, PARTIAL, OVERDUE, VOID. Data exchange (exports, APIs, integrations) always uses these upper-case codes exactly. The lower-case or translated labels shown in the user interface are display text only and must never be exported.\nLifecycle: created as DRAFT; issuing makes it SENT; payments make it PARTIAL or PAID; an unpaid invoice past its due date may be OVERDUE; issuing then cancelling without payments makes it VOID."
